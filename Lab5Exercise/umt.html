About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university in Malaysia that focuses on marine science and aquatic resources. Located in Kuala Terengganu, Terengganu, UMT has evolved from a small marine science station into a full-fledged university dedicated to advancing knowledge and fostering sustainable development of national marine wealth.

The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs spanning computer science, maritime technology, science, management, and maritime engineering. Through cutting-edge research, teaching excellence, and community engagement, UMT strives to be a prominent higher education institution recognized globally for its niche areas.
